# Changelog

## 0.2.0 — 10 November 2026 (author review)

* Three distinct demonstrations: sensible heating, drying–humidification and oxygen-limited self-heating.
* Exact diffusion, advection and first-order reaction verification.
* Repeated mesh/cost study with 160-cell references and full timing records.
* Sparse 15-color finite-difference Jacobian with fixed stored structure.
* Explicit material presets, p.user constants, kinetics and water-activity examples.
* Zero transport/heat coefficients supported for limiting problems.
* Extended tests, conservation checks and detailed extension guide.
* Six publication figures and editable source-code appendix in the manuscript.
* MATLAB execution, approved license and public release remain pending.

## 0.1.0 — 10 November 2026 (author review)

* Modular conservative one-dimensional heat, moisture and oxygen solver.
* Local reaction and exchange callbacks; piecewise inlet schedules.
* Explicit quantile events, water/energy diagnostics, MAT/CSV export.
* Five examples, 13 verification groups and Python reference fixtures.
* Executed using GNU Octave 7.3.0; MATLAB execution pending.
* License and public repository release require author completion.
